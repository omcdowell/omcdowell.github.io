# DinoBot

DinoBot is an iPhone app for chatting with AI helpers ("bots") that run on **your own computer**.
Your iPhone talks to your computer over [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com), a private network that only your own devices can join.
Your chats, files and settings stay on your phone and your computer.

DinoBot is in a small private test. There is no public download yet.
